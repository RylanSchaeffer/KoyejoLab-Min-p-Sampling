\section*{Impact Statement}

This work directly confronts the crisis of rigor in machine learning by establishing a blueprint for meticulous empirical evaluation. By introducing the ``Best-of-N'' protocol and demonstrating how unequal optimization and statistical flaws can manufacture illusory gains, we aim to realign community incentives toward scientific validity over leaderboard chasing. The primary impact of this research is to prevent the adoption of methods based on noise, thereby reducing wasted community resources and ensuring that future AI advancements are grounded in reproducible, statistically sound evidence.
